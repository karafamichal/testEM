Every bus in Slovakia, in your pocket

Plan&Ride shows live departures, plans your journey and follows your bus with a live notification, so you know when to leave and where to get off.

Coming soon to Google Play What's new

Free and open source. Works without an account.

Live departures

Buses at any stop with their real delay, plus the other carriers' intercity buses from the timetable.

Journey planner

Connections by city and intercity bus, on any day and time, with platforms and changes.

Follow your bus

A live notification and a card on the home screen count down to departure, show the next stop and tell you when to get off.

Will I catch it?

How long the walk to the stop is and when to leave. Your location stays on your phone.

Regular rides

Choose the days, and the app starts following your usual bus by itself before it leaves.

Your ticket

Optional: add your sadzv.qrbus.me account to show your QR ticket, credit and history, protected by PIN or fingerprint. Add credit right in the app; you pay on the operator's payment gateway and the money goes straight to SAD Zvolen.

Built with riders

Many buses don't report where they are. Riders who opt in can share where their bus is, with a tap or automatically while riding. Only the bus and the stop are shared, never anyone's location.

Plan&Ride is an unofficial app and is not affiliated with any transport operator.
