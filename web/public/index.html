Install testEM

testEM runs as an app. Add it to your Home Screen and open it from there to continue.

  1. Tap the Share button in the Safari toolbar.
  2. Scroll down and choose Add to Home Screen.
  3. Open testEM from your Home Screen.
  1. Tap the ⋮ menu in your browser.
  2. Choose Install app (or Add to Home screen).
  3. Open testEM from your Home Screen.

It must run from the Home Screen to work correctly.
